Family Grocery & Nutrition Relief

Targeted doorstep intervention for families in critical crisis: widows, sole mothers caring for paralyzed children, bedridden fruit vendors, and elderly citizens without family support across Ganeshnagar (Songadh) and Roopwada (Vyara).

Initiative 03

Summer Wildlife & Animal Care

During Gujarat's extreme summer temperatures where temperatures surpass 42°C, natural water sources dry up. Smit Foundation deploys eco-friendly baked clay water bowls strategically across neighborhoods to prevent stray animal and avian dehydration.

pets 40 Clay Bowls Placed 20 specialized wide bowls for street animals + 20 elevated shallow bowls for local birds and sparrows.
